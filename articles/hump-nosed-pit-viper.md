# Hump-nosed pit viper

*Hypnale hypnale* is a venomous pit viper found in India and Sri Lanka. People also call it the hump-nosed viper, Merrem's hump-nosed viper, or the Oriental hump-nosed viper.

## How people recognise it

It is a small, stout snake. The snout turns up, which gives the "hump-nosed" name. Like other pit vipers it has a heat-sensing pit between the eye and the nostril. The pupil is vertical. None of those marks is reliable in a blurry photo of the middle of the body.

## Why the camera may not name it

This species is in the field notes on the phone. It is not one of the classes in the quick model or in the 135-species catalog. A photo of this snake can still be given some other viper's name. Treat that as a miss, and use the field notes rather than the score alone.

## What a name in this app does not prove

Nothing the camera prints is a diagnosis.
