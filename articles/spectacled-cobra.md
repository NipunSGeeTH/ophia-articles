# Spectacled cobra

The spectacled cobra, *Naja naja*, is also called the Indian cobra. It is a venomous elapid of the Indian subcontinent, and one of the four snakes most often named in bite records from India and Sri Lanka.

## How people recognise it

When it feels threatened it can lift the front of the body and spread a hood. The hood often shows a pale mark that looks like a pair of spectacles, which is where the common name comes from. The eye is round, not a vertical slit.

A rat snake can also raise its front half. It does not spread a true hood, and the eye is larger. A photo of only the raised neck is a weak photo.

## Where it lives

It is native to the Indian subcontinent. It is often found near farms, houses, and the edges of fields, because those places hold the rodents it hunts. It also uses forest and scrub.
